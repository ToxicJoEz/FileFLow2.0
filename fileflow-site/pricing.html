FAQ

Common questions.

Is there a free trial? +
Yes — Pro comes with a 14-day free trial. No credit card required. Cancel anytime before the trial ends and you won't be charged.
Does FileFlow upload my files? +
Never. FileFlow runs 100% locally on your Windows PC. No file content ever leaves your machine — not even metadata.
What payment methods do you accept? +
We accept all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, Amex) via Stripe. All payments are encrypted and secure.
Can I switch plans later? +
Yes. You can upgrade or downgrade at any time from your billing dashboard. Upgrades take effect immediately, downgrades at the end of your billing period.
How does team billing work? +
Team pricing is per seat, billed monthly or annually. You can add or remove seats at any time and we prorate automatically.
Is there a student or nonprofit discount? +
Yes. Contact us with proof of student enrollment or nonprofit status and we'll set up a discounted plan for you.
